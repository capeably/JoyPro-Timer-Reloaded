---
name: feedback-quiet-controls
description: "Timer controls stay faded at rest. Read before styling the play, pause, skip or reset buttons."
type: feedback
---

Keep the timer controls (play/pause, skip, reset) faded at rest. They come up to full strength on hover or keyboard focus, and stay at full strength on touch screens.

**Why:** the time is the hero and the controls shouldn't compete with it. In the June 2026 audit of the original, Adam turned down raising their resting opacity for discoverability.

**How to apply:**

- Don't "fix" the faded look for discoverability; teach the controls through onboarding, tooltips and the shortcut hints instead.
- Keyboard focus must still bring the controls to full strength.
