          The time

          While paused, double-click the time to change it. Use the arrows, the mouse wheel (steps of five) or type. On a segment you haven’t started, that sets its length; part-way through, it changes only the time left. Double-click the segment title to rename it.

          The ribbon

          The colored ribbon at the bottom is your whole session, each segment sized by its length. Click a segment to jump to it. The colors run through the JoyPro spectrum, so a finished session paints the full logo.

          Editing a session

          Open Edit session to rename segments, change lengths, reorder them by dragging the grip (or focus the grip and use the arrow keys), and set each one’s chime, auto-advance and theme. Save, save a copy, or revert to the last save. All sessions loads, duplicates, imports and exports them.

          Themes

          Light and Dark are calm and plain. Starry Night twinkles, with the odd shooting star. In Growing Tree, a new bonsai grows through each segment while the day turns to night. Keep an eye out for visitors. Any segment can use its own theme.

          Sounds & the mini timer

          Each segment can chime when it ends: pick a built-in sound or upload your own (MP3 or WAV) in the Sound Library. The mini timer floats above your other windows where the browser allows it, with play and skip buttons of its own.
